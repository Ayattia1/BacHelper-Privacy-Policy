```
Account & Privacy

Account Deletion

This page explains how BacHelper users can delete their account and request deletion of associated online data.

Effective date: August 27, 2026
```

```
Your data, your choice. BacHelper allows users who sign in with Google to request deletion of their account and associated online data. You can delete your account directly from the app or contact us if you cannot access the app.
01

Delete Your Account from Inside the App

The easiest way to delete your BacHelper account is directly from the application.

1
Open BacHelper Launch the BacHelper application.
2
Open Settings Go to the Settings section.
3
Select Delete Account Permanently Tap the account deletion option.
4
Read the Warning Review the information displayed before continuing.
5
Confirm Deletion Confirm the deletion request as shown in the app.
Important: After confirmation, BacHelper will attempt to delete your Google-linked account data and sign you out.
02

Request Account Deletion by Email

If you cannot access BacHelper, you can request account deletion by contacting us.

Email us at:
aymen.attia.work@gmail.com
Request Account Deletion

Please include

  • The email address used to sign in with Google.
  • A clear request to delete your BacHelper account.
For security reasons, we may ask for additional information to verify that you own the account before processing the deletion request.
03

Data Deleted When You Delete Your Account

When account deletion is requested, BacHelper attempts to delete account-related online data, including:

User Profile Profile document, username, profile photo URL, and study division stored online.
Community Posts Posts created by your account.
Uploaded Images Images uploaded with your community posts.
Comments & Replies Comments and replies associated with your account.
Follow Relationships Follow relationships associated with your account.
Community Notifications Notifications related to your community account.
Reports Reports created by you, where deletion is allowed.
Moderation Records Ban or moderation records where deletion is allowed by security and moderation requirements.
04

Data That May Remain Temporarily

Some information may remain for a limited period when necessary for:

  • Security and abuse prevention.
  • Moderation history.
  • Legal compliance.
  • Technical limitations.
  • Backup or logging systems managed by third-party service providers.
Some content may also remain if it is not technically possible to identify or delete it immediately, or if it must be retained to investigate abuse or protect other users.
05

Guest Users

Guest users do not have a Google-linked BacHelper account.

Guest study data is stored locally on the device.

To delete guest data

  • Clear BacHelper app data from your device settings.
  • Or uninstall BacHelper from your device.
Local guest data may include study sessions, reminders, lessons, notes, Pomodoro progress, and app preferences.
06

Deleting Local Device Data

Deleting your Google-linked account removes online account data where possible. Local app data stored on your device may remain until you clear it.

To remove local BacHelper data

1
Open device Settings Open your Android device settings.
2
Go to Apps Open the list of installed applications.
3
Select BacHelper Open BacHelper's application settings.
4
Open Storage Open the application's storage settings.
5
Clear Data or Uninstall Clear the app data or uninstall BacHelper.

The exact steps may vary depending on your Android device.

07

Third-Party Services

BacHelper uses Google Sign-In and Firebase services. Some data may therefore be processed by Google according to Google's own privacy and retention policies.

More information

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
08

Contact

For account deletion requests, privacy questions, or support, contact BacHelper.

Need Help?

If you cannot delete your account from inside the application, send us an account deletion request by email.
```
